# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone, whatever their experience level, background or
  identity.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, trolling, or sharing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, code, or issues that break these rules, and may
block people who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem privately, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or
issue, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile
([@dracuthang2000](https://github.com/dracuthang2000)).
